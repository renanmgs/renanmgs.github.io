Game Design · Systems Design

Pulse Hero

A fitness RPG that turns real-world training into character progression — my current project, launching on Android and iOS.

Steps and workouts become XP, loot and progression. I design the game — classes, skills, combat, economy, progression and season structure — and build it end to end, from the Flutter client to a server-authoritative backend.

The design is documented and versioned: 40+ design documents covering classes, combat, dungeons, guilds, PvP, professions and monetization, plus a living balance sheet that is the single source of truth for tuning every class, skill and item.

13 Playable Classes
312 Skill-Tree Nodes

What I Design and Build

  • Class identity, skill trees and turn-based combat — 13 classes, 52 skills, 312 talent nodes
  • Progression, economy, crafting and season-pass design backed by a documented balancing method
  • Campaign, 11 dungeons, endless tower, co-op runs, guild expeditions and asynchronous PvP arena
  • Fitness loop: steps and workouts converted into XP, loot and missions with server-side anti-abuse caps
  • Server-authoritative backend (ASP.NET Core .NET 9, MongoDB, WebSocket combat) and a Flutter client for Android and iOS

Game Design · Level Design

A turn-based puzzle game inspired by Lara Croft GO, built and released at Rede Novo Tempo.

I was Game Designer, Level Designer, gameplay programmer and Unity engine tech on the project. I designed and built all of the game's levels, puzzle layouts, mechanics, progression and quests, and implemented grid-based movement, environmental interactions, player experience, UI/UX and the custom shader work.

The game shipped publicly. Development was interrupted when I moved into a mobile and web development role and could no longer maintain it — but it remains the project where I connected game mechanics with level structure, puzzle difficulty and player progression end to end.
